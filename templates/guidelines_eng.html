🎯 1. Artifact Definition

Image artifacts refer to:

Unintended, implausible, or visibly corrupted regions within images generated by diffusion models. These artifacts often break the natural semantics or visual coherence of an image, such as a person with extra fingers, a car with warped wheels, or missing parts of animals, and can significantly degrade image quality or realism.

✅ Our Focus: Structural Artifacts

  • Shape distortions: Objects with impossible geometry
  • Missing parts: Incomplete objects or body parts
  • Extra parts: Duplicated or additional elements
  • Merged objects: Things unnaturally fused together

❌ Not Considered Artifacts

  • Text distortions: Blurry or incorrect text
  • Stylistic errors: Art style inconsistencies
  • Physical implausibility: Unrealistic but structurally sound scenes
  • Prompt misalignment: Image doesn't match description

🏷️ 2. Artifact Categories

Addition

Definition: Excessive or repeated components leading to unrealistic objects.

Examples:

  • Person with 6+ fingers
  • Car with extra wheels
  • Animal with multiple heads
  • Duplicated body parts
Removal

Definition: Missing components that should be present.

Examples:

  • Person missing fingers
  • Car without wheels
  • Necessary components missing
  • Incomplete facial features
Distortion

Definition: Objects with atypical details that make them unrecognizable or broken.

Examples:

  • Warped faces or limbs
  • Twisted architectural elements
  • Scrambled object parts
  • Geometric inconsistencies
Fusion

Definition: Objects merged together with unclear boundaries.

Examples:

  • Two people merged into one
  • Objects blended together
  • Ambiguous hybrid parts
  • Indistinguishable elements

✅ 3. Classification Guidelines

Classification Process:

  1. Examine the entire image carefully
  2. Look for structural artifacts (not stylistic issues)
  3. Consider each artifact category
  4. Make your decision:
    • Has Artifacts - Clear structural defects present
    • No Artifacts - No structural issues found
    • Unsure - Uncertain cases for admin review

Key Principles:

  • Take your time - Accuracy over speed
  • Focus on structure - Not style or realism
  • Check all parts - Artifacts can be subtle
  • When in doubt - Use "Unsure" option
  • Reference examples - Compare with provided cases
Remember: Images that appear normal at first glance may contain subtle artifacts. Pay special attention to:
  • Hands and fingers, body parts
  • Faces and facial features
  • Architectural elements
  • Object boundaries

🎨 4. Annotation Guidelines

Drawing Bounding Boxes:

  • Click and drag to draw boxes
  • Make boxes tight around artifacts
  • One box per distinct artifact
  • Include complete artifact in the box

Selecting Categories:

  • Choose the primary category for each box
  • Be consistent with category definitions
  • Every box must have a category

Writing Captions:

  • Be specific and descriptive
  • Describe what's wrong
  • Keep it concise but informative
  • Every box must have a caption
