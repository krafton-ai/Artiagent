📖 Artifact Definition

Addition

Addition artifacts appear as excessive or repeated components, leading to unrealistic objects or implausible duplication

Removal

Removal artifacts appear as omitted components, leading to unrealistic or incomplete objects.

Distortion

Distortion artifacts occur when objects have details that are not typical, making the object unrecognizable or visually broken, such as geometric inconsistencies, abnormal textures, unnatural asymmetry or twisted, warped, scrambled parts.

Fusion

Fusion artifacts result from the combination of objects so their boundaries or interiors merge into one ambiguous, hybrid part, resulting in a visually incoherent region.

📋 Detailed Classification Guidelines

Our task focuses on structural artifacts, including distortions, duplicated or missing parts, and merged objects.

Text distortions, stylistic errors, physical implausibility and other natural image features are not considered artifacts in this task.

Check the provided prompts used for the image generation. Prompt alignment is not considered in this task.

Unrealistic images generated due to the prompts are not considered artifacts unless they contain structural errors.

Take your time and look carefully at all parts of the image.

Images seemingly natural at first glance may contain artifacts. Pay attention to the details and parts highly capable of containing artifacts.

If still unsure, mark as Unsure for admin review.
